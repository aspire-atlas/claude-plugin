# Outreach and Brief

## Job to be done

When creators are approved, I want a personal first message naming a real post of theirs, the offer for their deal type, the brief attached, follow-ups on a schedule, and replies sorted, so no creator goes cold and no one gets a generic message.

## Writes to Atlas

Write, one batch confirmation each: the message drafts and any creators moved to No reply, the email drafts created in the brand's own mailbox (nothing is sent), the recorded replies with each creator's new stage, and the outreach page link. Send, one confirmation per recipient: an email draft that already sits in the mailbox, unchanged, with no blank left, sent only when the person asked to send in this session. Direct messages and Aspire messages are never sent; the person sends them. A scheduled reply check saves nothing but the page link.

## Non-goals

- Choosing who to contact. That is 06 Creator Vetting, and 05 Creator Search for new names.
- Planning the campaign the brief belongs to. That is 11 Campaign Planner.
- Pricing a deal, answering a counter, or recording agreed terms. That is 14 Negotiator.
- Shipping product after a creator sends their details. That is 16 Product Fulfillment.
- Answering a creator's question about using their content. That is 26 Content Library and Rights.
- Setting up the program's offer, channels, and follow-up schedule. Program setup in `/aspire` does that.
