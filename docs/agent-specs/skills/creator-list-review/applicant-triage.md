# Applicant triage

## What it does

Sorts applicants to a program into who to contact now, who needs a closer look, and who to pass on.

## Inputs

- From brand memory: the vetting criteria and thresholds, or the criteria of the campaign the
  applicants are for.
- From the launch message: the applicant list as exported from the Aspire app or pasted, and its
  name and source.

## Output

The same recommendation per applicant as any list, with a page that opens on how many to
contact and by when, then the next steps by week. The final call stays with the person, and their
answers are saved through the feedback questions.

## Callable

Never user-callable. Only the agent that owns it, or every agent for a shared or universal skill,
runs it.
