# Positioning gap map

## What it does

Shows where competitors put creator money that the brand does not.

## Inputs

- From brand memory: the brand's rosters by tier, network, and content theme, and the market signal topics.
- From the launch message: the partnerships by competitor.

## Output

A grid of competitor against creator tier, network, and content theme, with the count of partners in each cell next to the brand's own count. Each cell where a competitor has three or more partners and the brand has none is called out as a gap, with two example creators from the net-new list. Gaps are described, never priced.

## Callable

Never user-callable. Only Competitor Roster runs it.
