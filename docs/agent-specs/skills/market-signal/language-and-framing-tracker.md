# Language and framing tracker

## What it does

Finds the short phrases creators use for each product and flags the ones that are spreading.

## Inputs

- From brand memory: the market signal topics and the last run's findings.
- From the launch message: the profile, the window, and the lenses (the messaging lens leads with it).

## Output

Per product: phrases of two to four words used by at least three creators, each with verbatim quotes and links, marked spreading when its creator count is at least double the baseline. For the messaging lens, also the gaps where the brand's positioning has no creator language behind it, and for the campaign lens, the language to borrow and what to avoid.

## Callable

Never user-callable. Only the agent that owns it, or every agent for a shared or universal skill,
runs it.
