# Audience and engagement read

## What it does

Reads who follows the creator and how their posts perform, using medians over up to 100 recent posts.

## Inputs

- From brand memory: the brand summary and goal, used only to frame whether the creator is relevant.
- From the launch message: the network and handle, and the recipient lens, which sets which numbers lead the page.

## Output

The facts tiles, the audience panel (gender, age bands, top locations), the KPI strip (median engagement rate, median view rate, posting cadence), the top five and lowest three posts by engagement rate, the engagement series of the 16 latest posts, and three to five sentences on what the numbers say. Each part is left out when its data is too thin, and named under data gaps.

## Callable

Never user-callable. Only the agent that owns it, or every agent for a shared or universal skill,
runs it.
