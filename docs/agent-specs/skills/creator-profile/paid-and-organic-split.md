# Paid and organic split

## What it does

Separates brands the creator was paid by from brands they only tagged or named.

## Inputs

- From brand memory: saved competitor and partner records, used only to mark a brand as a competitor or partner.
- From the launch message: the network and handle.

## Output

Two lists. Paid partnerships (confirmed): brands on posts marked as paid or clearly disclosed, plus the creator's past partners Atlas holds, with how many paid posts and how they were disclosed. Organic mentions (not confirmed as paid): other brands tagged or named with no disclosure. A competitor is labelled as one.

## Callable

Never user-callable. Only the agent that owns it, or every agent for a shared or universal skill,
runs it.
