# Reader-shaped sections

## What it does

Shapes the same analysis for each reader the brand saved, so leadership, product and growth each get the part they act on.

## Inputs

- From brand memory: `guideline:readout-audience`, the saved readers in order (team, leadership, product, product marketing, growth) with any destination saved for a reader, and the market signal findings for the week.
- From the launch message: the reader passed for this run, which leads, followed by the saved readers.

## Output

One section per reader on the same page, each with its own anchor, the lead reader first: leadership gets the outcome and trend, product gets competitor parity, product marketing gets messaging, growth gets the creators and formats to back. The chat summary is ordered for the lead reader, with two bullets per other reader and a forward note to paste.

## Callable

Never user-callable. Only the agent that owns it, or every agent for a shared or universal skill,
runs it.
