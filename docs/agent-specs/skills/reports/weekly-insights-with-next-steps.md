# Weekly insights with next steps

## What it does

Explains the patterns behind last week's results and ends with three ranked next steps.

## Inputs

- From brand memory: the same readout setup, `target:weekly-health` for the benchmark row, earlier daily, weekly and creator brief findings, the account review's content pushes, and last week's content reviews.
- From the launch message: the profile id, the run mode, an optional target week, and the reader the run is for.

## Output

A page comparing the week with the prior week and the 8-week median: the top three and bottom two posts with the pattern they share (format, theme, weekday, hook), the format and cadence mix, open action items with their age, and three ranked next steps. Patterns and next steps are saved as insights, and older action items now met are closed.

## Callable

Never user-callable. Only the agent that owns it, or every agent for a shared or universal skill,
runs it.
