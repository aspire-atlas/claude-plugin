# Quarterly one-pager for leadership

## What it does

Builds the quarter's one-page story for leadership: the business outcome first, the trend, the headline insights, and what to do next quarter.

## Inputs

- From brand memory: `brand:summary`, the 90-day goal, the lead metric, `target:weekly-health`, the competitors and `theme:brand`, plus the findings saved in the quarter and the one before: weekly and daily reports, creator briefs, content reviews, discovery, market signal, ad reuse, account reviews, post analyses and program snapshots.
- From the launch message: the quarter or dates, any spend the person typed, whether to show spend from the ledger, whether the person confirmed saving findings, and the reader (leadership by default).

## Output

One page: the outcome in one sentence with its number, three KPI tiles against the prior quarter, two or three headline insights, what each program delivered, three ranked recommendations, and the data gaps. A forward note the person can paste into a deck or email. Findings are saved only when the person confirmed, and never on a scheduled run.

## Callable

Never user-callable. Only the agent that owns it, or every agent for a shared or universal skill,
runs it.
