# Collab tracking

## What it does

Tracks each open and targeted collab from invite to accepted, sample shipped, video posted, and LIVE held.

## Inputs

- From brand memory: every `shop-campaign` finding for the brand.
- From the launch message: the period, and the collab records from Partner Center.

## Output

A stage per creator, the items overdue, and a chase draft for each.

## Callable

Never user-callable. Only TikTok Shop Campaigns runs it.
