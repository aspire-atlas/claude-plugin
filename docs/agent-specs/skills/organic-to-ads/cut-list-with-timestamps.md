# Cut list with timestamps

## What it does

Turns each top candidate into an edit-ready cut list and storyboard for creative.

## Inputs

- From brand memory: nothing beyond the hook score's inputs.
- From the launch message: the placements, when the person named them.

## Output

Per recommended ad: cuts with in and out timestamps, cut lengths and placements, and a storyboard on
the five beats every cut shares, with a still at every beat. Every timecode stays a draft until a
1-second frame scan confirms it. The page ends with a test plan: one variable per test, a
hypothesis, and a Scale, Fix or Stop rule.

## Callable

Never user-callable. Only the agent that owns it, or every agent for a shared or universal skill,
runs it.
