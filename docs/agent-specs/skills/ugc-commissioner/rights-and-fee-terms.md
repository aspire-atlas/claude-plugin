# Rights and fee terms

## What it does

Sets the usage, channels, duration and fee for each commissioned asset.

## Inputs

- From brand memory: the `rights` block in the program's terms (fees per 30 days) and the
  creator's agreed deal.
- From the launch message: the person's own number, when they gave one.

## Output

The usage, channels and duration per asset, and its pay: the person's number, product from the
catalog, or both, with its source. The program's usage fees price only the usage the asset grants,
never the asset itself. With no pay given, it is a `[fee]` blank and a your-call question. Nothing
the deal already grants is asked for again. No other creator's fee ever appears in a draft.

## Callable

Never user-callable. Only the agent that owns it, or every agent for a shared or universal skill,
runs it.
