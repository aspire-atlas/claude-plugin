# Creators and communities driving each pattern

## What it does

Names the creators and communities who started a pattern or carry most of its reach.

## Inputs

- From brand memory: the brand's rosters and campaign pools, to mark creators the brand already knows.
- From the launch message: the patterns and their posts.

## Output

Per pattern: the earliest creators to use it in the window and the top creators by engagement on it (handle, network, followers, engagement rate against their own median), each marked as on a roster, in a campaign pool, or new to the brand. The communities are named from the hashtags and topics the posts share. New creators are listed only, never fetched or saved; Creator Search (05) can take them as seeds.

## Callable

Never user-callable. Only Trend Scout runs it.
