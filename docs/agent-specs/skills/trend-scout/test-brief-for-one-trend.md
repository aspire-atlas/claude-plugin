# Test brief for one trend

## What it does

Turns one pattern the person picks into a small test the brand can run this cycle.

## Inputs

- From brand memory: the brand's guidelines and voice, and its performance baseline for the format.
- From the launch message: the pattern the person picked and its relevance, risk, and creators.

## Output

A one-page test brief: the pattern and why now, the angle for the brand, two to three post ideas, the format and length, what to avoid (from the risk score), one or two creators who could make it, a success target from the brand's own median for that format, and a date to judge it. It is a draft handed to Outreach and Brief (13); it saves nothing.

## Callable

Never user-callable. Only Trend Scout runs it.
