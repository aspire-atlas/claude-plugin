# Shipping and delivery tracking

## What it does

Follows each order from placed to delivered, from the store, pasted tracking, and the order form.

## Inputs

- From brand memory: every `fulfillment` finding for the program.
- From the launch message: tracking the person pasted, and the store connection.

## Output

A status per creator and the parcels past their delivery window. On delivery: the date the post is due, the stage move, and the hand-off list for Campaign Manager (15).

## Callable

Never user-callable. Only Product Fulfillment runs it.
