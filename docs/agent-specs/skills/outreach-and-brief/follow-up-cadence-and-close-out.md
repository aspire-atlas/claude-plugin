# Follow-up cadence and close-out

## What it does

Picks the creators who are due a follow-up or a close-out today and writes that next message.

## Inputs

- From brand memory: the program's outreach record (follow-up days, close-out day, channels, voice, sender) and the roster with each creator's stage, tier, last contact, and next follow-up date.
- From the launch message: the program, follow-up mode, and any creators named.

## Output

For each creator due, the next draft in the sequence on each channel, plus the creators past their reply window who should move to No reply. Creators with 100K or more followers get at least 7 days between messages and a close-out at least 21 days after the first touch. A second follow-up cites a different post from the first message when one qualifies.

## Callable

Never user-callable. Only the agent that owns it, or every agent for a shared or universal skill,
runs it.
