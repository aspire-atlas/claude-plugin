# Brief packaging

## What it does

Publishes the content brief creators work from as one visual page, for a week or for a dated campaign.

## Inputs

- From brand memory: `brand:summary`, red lines and guidelines, competitors, the campaign record and criteria, the campaign's discovery shortlist, saved market signal and readout findings, the `fees:rate-card` record, and the brand's page theme.
- From the launch message: the brief mode (week or campaign), the target week or campaign with its first post and end dates, the sourcing scope, the creator roles wanted, the budget posture, and the reader.

## Output

The brief page link, with the deliverables, guardrails, first-pick creators with their fees, and the timeline. Today outreach drafts are plain text with no attachment and no link to the brief, so the person shares the brief page with creators.

## Callable

Never user-callable. Only the agent that owns it, or every agent for a shared or universal skill,
runs it.
