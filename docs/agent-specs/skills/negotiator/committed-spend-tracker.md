# Committed spend tracker

## What it does

Adds up what agreed deals and open offers cost against the program budget.

## Inputs

- From brand memory: the newest terms for every creator on the roster, and the program budget.
- From the launch message: the deals proposed in this run.

## Output

Three figures: committed, offers out, and remaining against the budget, with commission shown as "plus commission on sales". Any move that would take committed spend over the budget becomes the person's call. With no budget set, only committed and offers out are shown.

## Callable

Never user-callable. Only the agent that owns it, or every agent for a shared or universal skill,
runs it.
