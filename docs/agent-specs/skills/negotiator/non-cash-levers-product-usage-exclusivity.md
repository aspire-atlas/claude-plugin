# Non-cash levers (product, usage, exclusivity)

## What it does

Proposes ways to close a gap that cost nothing outside what the program allows.

## Inputs

- From brand memory: the program's product value caps, posting window, end date, and affiliate commission.
- From the launch message: the creator's ask and the gap between their number and the room.

## Output

Up to one lever per counter, inside bounds: extra product within the cap, up to 14 more days to post, or commission at the program's standard rate. Shorter exclusivity, fewer deliverables, and trimmed usage are always the person's call, never proposed on the agent's word.

## Callable

Never user-callable. Only the agent that owns it, or every agent for a shared or universal skill,
runs it.
