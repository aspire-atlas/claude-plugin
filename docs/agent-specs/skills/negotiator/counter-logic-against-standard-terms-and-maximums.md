# Counter logic against standard terms and maximums

## What it does

Picks one move per creator: accept, counter with a number, add a lever, walk away, or hand the decision to the person.

## Inputs

- From brand memory: the program's standard terms and maximums, the roster, the newest reply and terms for the creator, saved team calls, competitors, and red lines.
- From the launch message: the mode, a pasted reply when there is one, the roster review's renewal picks for renewals, or the change asked for.

## Output

Per creator, the move with its number rounded to USD 10 and two reasons, plus the reply drafted in the right template. A number above the maximum, a change to the standard terms or deal type, or a deal over budget comes back as one question with the recommended answer first.

## Callable

Never user-callable. Only the agent that owns it, or every agent for a shared or universal skill,
runs it.
