# Quota delivered against agreed

## What it does

Counts each ambassador's posts delivered against the monthly quota in their deal.

## Inputs

- From brand memory: `program:{slug}-terms`, each creator's newest `terms` finding, and every `deliverable` finding.
- From the launch message: named ambassadors, or the whole roster.

## Output

Delivered against agreed per ambassador per month, with late and missing posts listed.

## Callable

Never user-callable. Only Ambassador Program runs it.
