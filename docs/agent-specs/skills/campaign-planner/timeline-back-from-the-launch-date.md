# Timeline back from the launch date

## What it does

Works the campaign's steps back from the first post date so every step has a due date.

## Inputs

- From brand memory: the campaign window from the campaign record.
- From the launch message: the first post date, the campaign end date, and today's date.

## Output

A dated timeline: outreach three weeks before the first post, confirmations two weeks before, brief calls, drafts due one week before, content review, final approvals, go live, a daily pulse through the end date, and a recap 7 and 14 days after. When the first post is closer than three weeks, the early steps are compressed and the risk is flagged. No step is ever scheduled in the past.

## Callable

Never user-callable. Only the agent that owns it, or every agent for a shared or universal skill,
runs it.
