# Measurement plan with targets from the brand's own medians

## What it does

Sets a numeric target for each deliverable from the brand's own post medians, and the measures to watch during and after the campaign.

## Inputs

- From brand memory: the campaign goal in the campaign record.
- From the launch message: the brand's linked handles and the lookback window (90 days by default), which set the brand's recent posts and their medians by format and theme.

## Output

Two tables. During: creator posts live, engagement against each creator's own median, mentions, and sentiment. After: the campaign goal against the recap. Each deliverable carries one target, such as a multiple of the brand's median Reel views.

## Callable

Never user-callable. Only the agent that owns it, or every agent for a shared or universal skill,
runs it.
