# Brand read from website and channels

## What it does

Reads the brand's own website, review sites, press and connected accounts to suggest answers, and shows them with sources before anything is saved.

## Inputs

- From brand memory: the brand summary and business context already saved, so it researches only what is missing, and the linked handles from the profile's channels.
- From the launch message: the profile, its linked handles and networks, and which question the person asked to research (brand summary, competitors, partners, tracking scope, voice or the weekly target).

## Output

Three to six findings per question, each with its source, for the person to record, edit or discard. For partners it also lists the tagged handles and collaboration language in the brand's own posts; for tracking scope, the hashtags the brand's posts and site use. A finding that is recorded is saved from the web; one the person edits is saved as their own answer. Nothing is saved straight from a search.

## Callable

Never user-callable. Only the agent that owns it, or every agent for a shared or universal skill,
runs it.
