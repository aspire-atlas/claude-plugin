# Disclosure and people-on-screen clearance

## What it does

Checks how the sponsor is disclosed, and lists who and what a reuse cut would need cleared.

## Inputs

- From brand memory: nothing.
- From the launch message: the post's link.

## Output

Whether the sponsor is named and whether a disclosure word appears, how often the account names sponsors in its other posts, and the platform's paid partnership label listed as a gap. Then everyone on screen with their role and seconds, named only when the post itself names them, and a checklist for the brand's legal team: likeness, trademarks, league or team marks, and whether the audio is original, licensed or unconfirmed.

## Callable

Never user-callable. Only the agent that owns it, or every agent for a shared or universal skill,
runs it.
