# Brands on screen, timed to the second

## What it does

Finds every brand or mark seen or heard in the post and times each stretch it is on screen.

## Inputs

- From brand memory: saved competitor and partner records, to mark a brand the requesting brand cares about.
- From the launch message: the post's link.

## Output

One entry per brand: its group (paid sponsor, wardrobe, show or founder, league or team, background), a frame still with its time, total seconds on screen and seconds legible, and its time ranges, drawn as a timeline. It also lists marks it could not identify, and every disagreement with the brands Atlas lists for the post.

## Callable

Never user-callable. Only the agent that owns it, or every agent for a shared or universal skill,
runs it.
