# Brand safety across 12 categories, profanity timed

## What it does

Rates the post on the 12 industry brand safety categories and times each instance of profanity.

## Inputs

- From brand memory: saved red lines, each checked as its own row when it bears on the post.
- From the launch message: the post's link, and the recipient lens.

## Output

An overall read (Suitable, Suitable with conditions, or Not suitable as posted), a risk level per category with its evidence and Atlas's own rating beside it where they differ, an instance log (the line as censored, speaker, on screen or not, time), the cleanest trim with what it loses, and a fit by advertiser risk tolerance in three tiers.

## Callable

Never user-callable. Only the agent that owns it, or every agent for a shared or universal skill,
runs it.
