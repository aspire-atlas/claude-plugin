# Budget pacing to end of term

## What it does

Works out where the program stands against its budget and what it is on track to spend by the end of the term.

## Inputs

- From brand memory: the program budget and term, ambassador monthly fees and product allowances for months to come, and the offers still out on the newest `terms` findings.
- From the launch message: the program slug and the reader the run is for.

## Output

Per currency, never converted: budget, paid, owed, committed, product cost, spent and committed, remaining, offers out, forecast to term end, and remaining after offers. Breakdowns by type, by month and by creator, and a burn chart by month. Amounts go only in the team view of the page, never in its shell or a forward note.

## Callable

Never user-callable. Only the agent that owns it, or every agent for a shared or universal skill,
runs it.
