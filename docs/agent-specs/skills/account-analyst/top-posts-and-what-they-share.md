# Top posts and what they share

## What it does

Finds each account's best posts by engagement and names what they have in common.

## Inputs

- From brand memory: the brand summary and goals, to say why a pattern matters.
- From the launch message: the profile, the handles in scope, and the reader lens.

## Output

The top three posts per account with the pattern they share and the evidence post for each, plus a visual payload: the top five and bottom three posts with links, media, dates, format, a caption excerpt and their numbers, and the per-period series behind the headline, under 60 rows, for `/aspire` to show as cards and a chart.

## Callable

Never user-callable. Only the agent that owns it, or every agent for a shared or universal skill,
runs it.
