# Ad reuse score when the reader is paid or creative

## What it does

Scores the one post for reuse as an ad when the reader is a paid or creative team, or the person asks whether it can run as an ad.

## Inputs

- From brand memory: nothing beyond what the review already read.
- From the launch message: the recipient lens (performance or creative) or the person's question about running the post as an ad.

## Output

The hook score, the hook pattern, the best cut with timestamps, and the rights line, scored from the frames the review already pulled. It is advice and never changes the verdict. A draft has no views yet, so its retention is listed as missing.

## Callable

Never user-callable. Only the agent that owns it, or every agent for a shared or universal skill,
runs it.
