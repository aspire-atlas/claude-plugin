# U4 Market and competitor signal

## What it does

Answers how the brand is talked about against competitors, and what the real threat is.

## Inputs

- From brand memory: the brand's name, handles, and tracked hashtags; each tracked competitor with handles and spelling variants; the tracked topics and the words creators use for them; and the last run's findings.
- From the launch message: the window (with a four-week baseline) and the reader.
- For a roll-up (reports): the saved market signal findings for the period, read as they are and never re-run.

## Output

Share of voice per brand (posts, distinct creators, engagement), organic and paid apart, with the change in points; each feature marked won, lost, even, or unclear against each competitor, with creator counts and quoted proof; friction users mention; phrases that are spreading; what changed since the last run; and the gaps (capped sets, competitors Atlas does not index).

## Callable

Never user-callable. Agents that list U4 under **Universal skills used** run it, and it is never
counted in an agent's own skills.
