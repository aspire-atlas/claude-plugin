# U6 Rights and usage

## What it does

Answers what the brand may run, and what it needs to ask for.

## Inputs

- From brand memory: the program's usage, whitelisting, and exclusivity terms and its usage fee per 30 days by kind of usage.
- From the content: the people and marks on screen and the audio type (original, licensed, or unconfirmed).
- From the launch message: the brief's rights field when a draft is reviewed against a brief.

## Output

A clearance checklist (likeness, trademarks, league or team marks, audio); the rights line on an ad reuse score; and a pro-rata usage fee for adding or extending usage, or "the person's number" when no fee is saved. Rights read from a post alone are always "Can't check", and any change to usage terms is marked as the person's decision.

## Callable

Never user-callable. Agents that list U6 under **Universal skills used** run it, and it is never
counted in an agent's own skills.
