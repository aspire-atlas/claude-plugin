Our posts with no paid label out-engage Pine and Pebble; TikTok Shop selling posts pull the average down

For marketing leadership | Brightside Toys against Lumen Kids and Pine and Pebble | Oct 1 to Oct 31, 2026 | TikTok and Instagram

The answer

Creator posts about Brightside Toys with no paid label earned 14.7 engagements per 1,000 views, ahead of Pine and Pebble at 12.9. TikTok Shop selling posts earned 3.6. Lumen Kids is not quiet: creators posted about it 20 times, mostly without tagging it.

How it was worked out

A post counts as a mention when it tags the brand, writes its @handle, names the brand or a product in the caption, or says or shows the name in the video. Posts are split into three kinds: paid label, TikTok Shop selling, and no paid label. A figure from 20 posts or fewer is marked directional.
